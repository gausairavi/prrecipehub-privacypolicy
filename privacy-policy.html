Privacy Policy for PR Recipe Hub

Effective Date: Aug 31, 2026 | Last Updated: Auly 31, 2026

1. Overview & Privacy Commitment

Welcome to PR Recipe Hub ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to maintaining transparency regarding how your data is handled when using our mobile application (the "Application").

PR Recipe Hub is designed as an offline-first application. Your personal culinary data—including saved favorite recipes, weekly meal plans, kitchen pantry inventory, grocery lists, and cooking preferences—is stored locally on your device.

2. Information Collection & Usage

A. Local Device Storage

All user-generated content (favorites, pantry inventory, meal schedules, and timers) remains 100% on your device using encrypted local storage. We do not upload your personal recipe data to external servers.

B. Public Recipe Data API (TheMealDB)

The Application fetches public culinary recipes and categories from TheMealDB API via secure HTTPS queries (https://www.themealdb.com). No personally identifiable information (PII) is included in these network queries.

C. Diagnostics & Performance Metrics

We may collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic information (such as app version, device model, operating system version, and crash logs) solely to maintain app stability and performance.

3. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

PR Recipe Hub uses Google AdMob to display advertisements to users on the free tier.

  • Google UMP & GDPR Compliance: For users in the EEA/UK, explicit consent for ad personalization is requested via the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog.
  • Ad Personalization Choices: You can grant, modify, or revoke ad consent at any time inside the app under Settings > Storage & Privacy.
  • Non-Personalized Ads: If you decline personalized advertising, contextual non-personalized ads are displayed instead.

4. Child Safety & COPPA Compliance

PR Recipe Hub is designed for general audiences and strictly complies with the Google Play Families Policy and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • The Application supports Child-Directed Treatment toggles in settings, instructing Google AdMob to disable interest-based advertising and remarketing.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

5. User Rights & Data Control

  • Data Portability: You can export a full JSON backup of your favorites, pantry items, and meal plans under Settings > Storage & Privacy.
  • Data Deletion & Cache Clearance: You can wipe cached recipe data or perform a complete data reset directly within the application settings at any time.

6. Permissions Requested

PR Recipe Hub requests minimal system permissions:

  • INTERNET: Needed to fetch online recipe content and render sponsor advertisements.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Optional permission used locally for meal prep alerts and cooking timers.

We do not request access to your precise GPS location, camera, contacts, or microphone.
